THE DETAILS MATTER.

Before you ask.

How does the free consultation work?

Send a request with a few details about your business and preferred availability. We’ll arrange a 20-minute call to discuss the problem, possible approaches, and budget. There’s no charge or obligation. Submitting a request does not reserve a calendar time.

What do the setup fee and monthly price cover?

Every package combines a one-time setup fee with an ongoing monthly service. The setup fee covers the agreed build and launch. The monthly price covers the stated checks, reporting, and change allowance. Hosting, domains, software licences, user seats, and usage fees are separate and identified before you commit.

When does monthly billing start, and can I cancel?

Monthly billing starts when the agreed setup goes live. The service is month-to-month with 30 days’ notice to cancel. Setup is paid separately, so there is no remaining build-financing balance. We agree on ownership and handoff in your proposal. Monthly checks, reports, and support stop when the service ends.

What if I need more changes than my plan includes?

We’ll explain the extra scope and quote it before starting. You can also move to a larger plan by agreement. Unused monthly change time does not roll over. The plans cover scheduled checks and business-hours support, not unlimited work or emergency response.

Does Website Relaunch cover my whole website?

It includes a full design and rebuild of up to five standard pages using your existing branding and supplied content, with light content editing. Larger websites, new brand identities, full copywriting, stores, and complex migrations need a tailored quote.

What is the difference between the two portal packages?

Portal Starter is a branded, template-based space for viewing existing project data and files from one source. Connected Portal adds integrations and one request or approval workflow. Both use an established platform. Website rebuilding and third-party licences are separate.

Who owns the website and software accounts?

Your website assets and client-specific configuration are handed over as agreed in the proposal. Third-party accounts should be registered to your business, and their licences remain subject to each provider’s terms.

Are results or launch dates guaranteed?

The consultation is free. Project pricing and timing depend on scope, content, access, and review turnaround. We agree on those details before work starts. We don’t guarantee revenue, lead volume, or a specific time-saving result.
